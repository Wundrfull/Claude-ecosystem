30 seconds. Read verbatim. This is Anthropic, not me, not you. Let it sit. "Context rot" is the term — use it when you talk to the team.

The rule. Read it. Put it on a sticky note.

When to /clear, when to /compact, when to /context.

If the next thing you are going to do is unrelated to what you just did → /clear.

If it is related and you need continuity → /compact.

If you are not sure → /context first, then decide.

Quit and re-launch only when you suspect the session picked up a bad assumption. /compact will summarize the bad assumption into the summary and it persists.

75 seconds. Read the rule line by line. Don’t paraphrase. The quit-and-relaunch line is the one most people skip — /compact can bake a wrong turn into the summary, and then you’re stuck with it.

What survives · and one budgeting gotcha

Not as much as you think.

What survives /compact

  • Skills re-attach, but each is capped at the first 5K tokens.
  • Inside a 25K total budget for skill re-hydration.
  • Huge skill files lose their tails. Keep skills short.

Opus 4.7 tokenizer

  • Up to 35% more tokens for the same text vs 4.6.
  • The new tokenizer is chunkier. Daily spend went up by default.
  • Budget for it.

Auto-compaction fires at ~95% of context. Override earlier with CLAUDE_CODE_AUTOCOMPACT_PCT_OVERRIDE=80.

75 seconds. Two takeaways on one slide. Left: /compact is not free — big skills get their tails cut. Right: if you migrated from 4.6 to 4.7 and your bill jumped, this is why.

The override at the bottom: 80 is a sensible value for long-running work. Forces summarization before you hit the wall.
